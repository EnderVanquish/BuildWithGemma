This camera watches the FRONT ENTRANCE / PORCH of a private home. It is an outdoor
entryway, not an indoor hallway. At night the camera switches to infrared, so the image
is dark, grainy and black-and-white — that is normal and is not itself suspicious.

What is expected here:
- Residents entering and leaving, at any hour.
- Delivery couriers approaching, leaving a package on the porch, and departing within
  roughly a minute, typically between 09:00 and 18:00.
- Packages sitting on the porch after a delivery, sometimes for hours, until a resident
  collects them by taking them INTO the house.

What is NOT expected, and should be treated as serious:
- Anyone picking up a package and carrying it AWAY from the house, out toward the street
  or driveway, rather than inside. This is package theft and is the single most important
  thing this camera exists to catch.
- A package that was present in earlier observations and is absent in a later one, with
  an unfamiliar person nearby in between.
- An unfamiliar person approaching the entrance late at night or in the early hours.
- Someone lingering near the entrance without approaching the door, looking around, or
  leaving and returning repeatedly over a short period.
- Anyone appearing to check whether they are being observed before acting.
